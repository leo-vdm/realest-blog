Aesthetic Rant

The aesthetic for this site is taken from Windows 7 (the last good Windows edition). More generally this style is called "Frutiger Aero", a descendant of the Y2K design aesthetic. Fruitiger Aero is characterised by these kinds of "glossy" design elements and lots of transparency, bright color and rounded edges. Personally I found it refreshing to have a design style with some color and intricate elements, since most designs these days are very much in the direction of minimalism. An especially striking thing looking back at Y2K and Fruitiger Aero UIs is the icons. They are not only more complex than modern icons (see example below) but also have unrestricted colors.



In most modern UIs, everything complies to a single (limited) color pallette. In the Y2K era styles each icon is as if it was designed to be an independant illustration. This can probably be explained as designers making the best of the new advancements in graphics tech at the time allowing them to break away from paletised colors into full 8-bit RGBA, but I also just think it looks better.
